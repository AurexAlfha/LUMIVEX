User: What is a language model? Assistant: A language model learns patterns in text and uses those patterns to predict or generate sequences of tokens. Language is a system used by people to communicate ideas, information, questions, and experiences. Clear writing uses understandable words and logical structure. Learning improves when information is organized into meaningful examples. LUMIVEX is an experimental language model project. A language model learns statistical patterns from sequences of tokens. Tokens are the basic units processed by a neural language model. The model uses context to predict what token should come next. Good training data should be diverse, clean, relevant, and legally usable. Artificial intelligence systems learn from examples. Neural networks contain parameters that are adjusted during training. Training minimizes a loss function by updating model parameters. Validation data is kept separate from training data. Evaluation helps measure whether a model generalizes beyond its training examples. LUMIVEX will be developed gradually.
